  1. Unit: dense FP16 TFLOPS, the rate most model inference runs at. NVIDIA and Apple publish it. The pocket AI boxes publish sparse INT8 TOPS. Conversion: INT8 to FP16 halves the rate; removing the sparsity assumption halves it again. 160 to 275 sparse INT8 TOPS is therefore 40 to 70 dense FP16 TFLOPS. The 275 figure includes two fixed-function accelerators that do not run general model math. The table uses ~40.
  2. 36 GB of unified memory holds about 70 billion parameters at 4-bit weights, with room for context. The 128 GB configuration holds proportionally more.
  3. Figures as published by the vendors in September 2026. The pocket AI box column covers two current crowdfunded pocket AI computers, and ranges span both. Product names belong to their owners.
