Solutions for industries and professionals

Creative artists Local AI Smart home Robotics Virtual reality

WiCi One is one GPU on a shelf, reached over Wi‑Fi by every device in the building. Five workloads we build it for: local AI, creative artists, robotics, virtual reality, and the smart home.

  1. Local AI Trillion-parameter models on hardware you own. Ollama, LM Studio, and PyTorch see a local GPU; agents call a standard endpoint. Private and real-time.
  2. Creative artists One GPU for the whole studio. Render, generate, and edit from lightweight machines. Blender, Premiere Pro, CapCut, and ComfyUI see a standard local GPU.
  3. Robotics On-site GPU inference for robots. Models too large for an on-robot processor, one wireless hop away, with no internet in the control loop and no video leaving the building.
  4. Virtual reality Move the compute off your head. Rendering, scene understanding, and AI on a nearby GPU, so the headset carries less compute, cooling, and battery.
  5. Smart home A home that understands what it sees. Supported cameras and devices feed a nearby GPU for live understanding and searchable home context, processed on the premises.
