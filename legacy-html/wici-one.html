The Big Idea

Let the GPU come to you.

Introducing WiCi One, a desktop-class GPU for your phone, tablet, or laptop. Our proprietary wireless computing technology delivers it over Wi-Fi, so the apps you already use, from local AI models to 3D rendering and 4K video editing, run in real time on your own hardware, connected to a wireless GPU. Not remote play or a remote connection.

Not Device-only. Not Cloud-only. Nearby.

Devices stay light and cool. No latency, no subscriptions, your data stays home. WiCi One is shared compute every device reaches wirelessly.

Developer Experience

A wireless GPU runtime
for local AI apps.

WiCi One is a programmable endpoint on the wireless compute layer: run existing apps unmodified in Driver mode, build with the WiCi SDK, or call it through the API. At every altitude, your app keeps interaction, input, and UI on the device while GPU-bound inference offloads over Wi-Fi, while model caching, streaming, and pipelining keep it real-time.

  1. Virtual GPU driver

    Attach WiCi One as a virtual GPU, and existing applications run unmodified, fully transparent.

  2. LLM-optimized SDK

    Build with full control: PyTorch and multimodal pipelines on wici.gpu, while I/O stays on the device.

  3. Drop-in API with trillion-parameter model support

    Keep your existing client and point it at WiCi One's OpenAI-compatible local endpoint.
