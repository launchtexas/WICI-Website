Privacy Policy

This Privacy Notice for WiCi Technologies, Inc. (doing business as WiCi) describes how and why we may access, collect, store, use, and share personal information when you use our Services.

This Privacy Notice applies when you visit https://wici.ai or any website of ours that links to this notice; download or use WiCi One, our Facebook application, or any other application of ours that links to this notice; or engage with us through related marketing, events, or support.

If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you have questions or concerns, contact us at [email protected].

WiCi One mobile app

This section describes WiCi One, published by WiCi Technologies, Inc. on Google Play as ai.wici.one. It clarifies the app's data practices separately from the website and other services described below.

  • Google sign-in: If you choose to sign in, Google provides your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information. The account service used by your app version processes that information to provide sign-in and account management. Original WiCi One 0.4.2 and build 21 retain the original account service; they do not move you into the replacement account service used by build 19. WiCi does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or calendar. Account information is not used for advertising or model training.
  • Photos and AI requests: When you use Box features, selected photos, prompts, and voice audio are sent to the WiCi Box selected in the app and results are returned to your device. Connect your phone and Box to the same Wi-Fi network. Original WiCi One 0.4.2 and build 21 also retain the original Album hosted-backend and fallback paths: if a Box request fails or no Box is available, an Album operation may use the existing hosted service according to the app's original behavior. Content needed for that operation is sent to the selected service. Your entire photo library is not automatically uploaded. Build 19's Box-only behavior is different.
  • Microphone and camera: These are used when you grant permission and invoke the relevant feature, including voice or a Mini App. Voice audio and any selected media are processed by the service used for that request. You can revoke permissions in Android settings.
  • AI-content reports: The report action sends only the description you submit, the feature name, and a random receipt ID to WiCi's Supabase reporting service. The service records the submission time and review status. Reports do not automatically include your sign-in identifier, photos, audio, or full conversation. Avoid including private information that is unnecessary to investigate the report. WiCi reviews reports for safety and abuse handling.
  • Device and service records: App settings, cached content, and local conversations may be stored on your device or Box. Hosting providers process request information, including IP addresses and operational logs, to deliver and secure their services. Account and report requests use HTTPS. Connections to a user-selected local Box may use HTTP on the local network; the app does not claim that every connection is encrypted.

The app's sign-in and reporting information is not sold or shared for advertising. Supabase and Google process the information needed to provide their services. Content you deliberately send to a Mini App or a separately configured backend is also governed by that service's practices. Photos, audio, prompts, and report text can contain personal or sensitive information you choose to provide.

Original WiCi One 0.4.2 and build 21 retain the original account service. The separate replacement service used by build 19 does not automatically import accounts or cloud data from that original service. Account information is kept while the account exists. Reports are retained for moderation and active abuse investigations; request removal using the receipt ID. Local files remain until you remove them. Routine hosting security logs follow the providers' retention settings.

Delete your WiCi One account and data

To request deletion of your WiCi One account and associated data, email [email protected] with the subject WiCi One account deletion, your sign-in email, and the account/data you want removed. This request path covers the original account service as well as later versions. Do not send passwords or login codes. We will verify ownership and confirm the outcome. You do not need to install the app or create a new account to make this request.

Open WiCi One account deletion for these instructions and the separate self-service control. Self-service deletion applies only to accounts in the replacement account service used by build 19. It does not delete an account in the original v0.4.2 service. For original accounts, use the email request above. In the app, open 3D Album settings and choose Request account deletion.

Deletion removes your WiCi sign-in account, Google identity association, and the email and profile information held in that account service. It does not delete your Google account, files on your phone or Box, or anonymous AI-content reports. You can remove local files using your device or Box controls and request report deletion by providing the report receipt ID.

If legal or security obligations require retaining specific information, we will explain the reason and retention period in our response.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you express interest in our products or Services, participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise contact us. This may include names, email addresses, usernames, and passwords.

Information collected automatically when you submit a form

When you submit a newsletter, waitlist, developer-access, or contact form, we automatically record the submission time, the form used, your time zone, the external referring domain, allowlisted campaign parameters such as UTM source, campaign, and content, coarse device, operating-system, and browser categories, and your approximate country, state or region, and city derived from your IP address. We link this information to the email address you submit so we can respond to inquiries, understand form sources, measure campaigns and advertising performance, and tailor the timing of requested communications.

Our edge service processes your IP address to derive this approximate location, but we do not include the IP address in the stored form record. We do not use our public forms to collect precise location, full referring URLs or arbitrary query parameters, raw user-agent strings, hardware identifiers, screen fingerprints, or similar fingerprinting data.

We do not process sensitive information. If you choose to make purchases, we may collect payment data necessary to process your payment, such as payment instrument number and security code. Payment data is handled and stored by Stripe. See Stripe's privacy notice.

If you register or log in using a social media account, we may collect profile information from that social media provider as described in the social logins section below.

If you use our applications, we may request access or permission for geolocation information; mobile device features such as Bluetooth, calendar, camera, contacts, microphone, reminders, sensors, SMS messages, social media accounts, storage, and other features; mobile device data such as device ID, model, manufacturer, operating system, browser type, carrier, IP address, and application feature usage; and push notifications. You can change these permissions in your device settings.

All personal information you provide must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information collected from other sources

In Short: We may collect limited data from public databases, marketing partners, social media platforms, and other outside sources.

We may obtain mailing addresses, job titles, email addresses, phone numbers, intent data, IP addresses, social media profiles, social media URLs, and custom profiles for targeted advertising, event promotion, and improving our records.

If you interact with us on a social media platform, we may receive information such as your name, email address, and gender. Information collected from your social media account depends on your account settings and is also governed by that platform's own privacy notice.

When you use our Facebook application, we may access basic account information such as your name, email, gender, birthday, current city, and profile picture URL, plus other information you choose to make public. We may request additional Facebook permissions such as friends, check-ins, and likes, which you may grant or deny.

12. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and applications include a Do-Not-Track feature or setting. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. We do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or similar automatic mechanisms. If a standard is adopted that we must follow, we will update this notice.

California law requires us to explain how we respond to DNT signals. Because there is no current industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
