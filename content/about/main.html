Then & now

The oldest new idea.

"There is no reason for any individual to have a computer in his home."

Ken Olsen, founder of Digital Equipment Corporation, 1977

It's called the most famous wrong prediction in computing, but DEC's founder swore he was misquoted. What he doubted was one central computer running the whole home, and for fifty years he was right: every device carried its own compute, and a computer for the home had no job to do.

His own VT100 knew better. It was just a screen on a desk, drawing on one powerful machine shared by everyone in the building. Personal AI finally gives that idea its job: your devices are learning to see, listen, think, and remember, and they need more power than they can carry. WiCi One is that machine, one serious GPU in your home, serving every device wirelessly. No data center, no wires.
