About WiCi

Company background & facts

WiCi is a systems research lab. We focus on wireless GPU computing and local inference systems for frontier AI. We develop systems for sharing compute across devices while keeping models and data under local control.

WiCi was founded by systems researchers and university faculty with a decade of award-winning work on real-time wireless networking and machine-learning systems, and is a member of NVIDIA Inception.

WiCi One, the first wireless GPU, brings desktop-class GPU compute to compatible devices over Wi-Fi.

WiCi Infer is a free desktop app for local AI, powered by our most capable local inference engine, InferSSD. It runs on your computer without needing to buy new hardware.

Overview

Product brief, specifications & pricing

A desktop-class NVIDIA GPU that sits on a shelf, joins your Wi‑Fi, and lends its power to the phone, laptop, or headset already in your hands. AI runs locally and privately, and nothing you carry gets heavier or hotter.

  • Local AI. Voice assistants that answer without a trip to the cloud, copilots on your own files, trillion-parameter models in Ollama or LM Studio, image and video generation in ComfyUI.
  • Your apps, more GPU. In Driver mode the computer sees WiCi One as its own graphics card, so Blender, Unreal, Premiere Pro, and games run on your machine. A wireless GPU, not remote play.
  • What comes next. AI gadgets and robots that keep the model on the same network as their cameras and sensors, and stay small and cool because the GPU lives nearby.

From $3,499 (RTX 5060 Ti edition). The RTX 5090 edition is $12,999.
