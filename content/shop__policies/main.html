Shipping, returns, and warranty

The short version of what happens after you buy WiCi One.

Shipping Returns & refundsReturns Warranty

Shipping

  • When it ships. Orders ship within 8 weeks of purchase. We email you a tracking number when yours is on its way.
  • Where we ship. Addresses in the United States.
  • Cost. Shipping and sales tax are calculated at checkout.
  • Changing your address. Email contact@wici.ai before your order ships.
  • Damaged or missing package. Keep the packaging and email us within 7 days of delivery. We will make it right.

Returns & refunds

  • Before it ships. You can cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships. Email contact@wici.ai with your order number.
  • After it ships. All sales are final. We do not accept returns or offer refunds for a change of mind.
  • If it arrives defective. Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number, a description of the problem, and photos or a video. We reply within 2 business days and replace the unit, with shipping both ways on us.

This covers defects and damage present on delivery, not damage or misuse afterward. Nothing here limits the rights you have under consumer law.

Warranty

  • How long. One year from the delivery date.
  • What is covered. Defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. We repair or replace the unit and pay shipping for approved claims.
  • What is not. Drops, impacts, and liquid; misuse; unauthorized modifications, repairs, or firmware; damage from third-party parts or power accessories; normal cosmetic wear.
  • How to claim. Email contact@wici.ai with your order or serial number and a description of the problem.
  • Your data. Service can reset or replace storage. Back up anything you keep on the device first.

This limited warranty is from WiCi Technologies, Inc. and is in addition to the rights you have under consumer law.

Questions? Email contact@wici.ai. We answer within 2 business days.
