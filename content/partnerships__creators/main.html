Introducing WiCi One

Desktop GPU power over Wi-Fi

WiCi One connects your device to a nearby NVIDIA RTX 5060 Ti with 16 GB of GPU memory. Your applications and project files stay on your device, while GPU processing runs on WiCi One.

  • Work wirelessly

    Access the GPU over your local Wi-Fi network without connecting your device to a dock.

  • Keep projects on your device

    Continue working with your local files without moving projects to a separate render machine.

  • Share compute across your studio

    Multiple devices can use one WiCi One, with project data staying on your network.

How co-creation works

We provide a WiCi One, help with setup from our founding engineers, and a media kit with product renders and footage to support your video.

  1. 01

    Discuss your project

    We’ll start with a 20-minute call to learn about your work, the tools you use, and the project you’d like to try.

  2. 02

    Agree on the details

    Before we ship your unit, we’ll agree on compensation, the video format and timing. We’ll also discuss the scope of our co-creation.

  3. 03

    Create and share

    Use WiCi One on your project with support from our team, then publish one video showing your workflow.
