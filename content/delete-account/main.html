Delete your WiCi One account

To request deletion of your WiCi One account and associated data, email adam@wici.ai with the subject WiCi One account deletion, your sign-in email, and the account/data you want removed. This request path covers the original account service as well as later versions. Do not send passwords or login codes. We will verify ownership and confirm the outcome. You do not need to install the app or create a new account to make this request.

Self-service deletion for build 19 accounts

Self-service deletion below applies only to accounts in the replacement account service used by build 19. It does not delete an account in the original v0.4.2 service. For original accounts, use the email request above.

Photos, conversations, and other data

Deleting this account does not erase photos or conversations saved on your phone or your WiCi Box. Remove those using the app, your photo library, or your Box controls. Signing in again creates a new account.

AI-content reports are submitted without a sign-in account identifier. To request deletion of a report or information you included in it, email us with its receipt ID. Reports needed for an active safety investigation may be retained until that investigation is resolved; we will explain any retention that applies to your request.

Cannot sign in, or need other data removed?

Email adam@wici.ai with the subject WiCi One account deletion, your WiCi sign-in email, and which account or data you want removed. This also covers requests concerning the previous account service. Do not send your password or login codes. We will verify ownership and confirm the outcome.

The account service keeps your account until it is deleted. Routine service security logs may remain under the hosting provider's retention settings. Where legal or security obligations require retaining specific information, we will explain the reason and retention period in our response.
